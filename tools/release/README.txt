SmartKeyboard
=============

Completes words as you type, fixes typos, predicts the next word, and learns
the words you use. It works in its own window and in the other apps on your
PC. Everything stays on your PC: nothing is sent anywhere.

Needs: Windows 10 or 11 on a normal Intel or AMD PC (x64).
Does not need: .NET or anything else. It is all in the "app" folder.


Installing
----------

1. Extract the whole zip. (Right click it, Extract All.)
2. Open the extracted folder and double click "Install SmartKeyboard.cmd".
3. That's it. A SmartKeyboard icon appears on your desktop and in the Start
   menu, it starts with Windows, and it is already running in the tray by
   the clock.

"Windows protected your PC"?
   SmartKeyboard is not signed with a paid certificate, so Windows warns
   about it the first time. Click "More info", then "Run anyway".
   To avoid the warning altogether: before extracting, right click the zip,
   choose Properties, tick "Unblock", and click OK.


Using it
--------

- Double click the desktop icon to open the SmartKeyboard window.
- Closing the window keeps it working from the tray. Right click the tray
  icon for the menu: pause it (Ctrl+Alt+K), settings, or exit.
- Fixing typos in OTHER apps starts switched off, because it changes text in
  apps it does not own. Turn it on from the tray menu: "Fix typos in other
  apps".
- Turn "Start with Windows" on or off from the tray menu or Settings.


Updating and removing
---------------------

- To update, extract the new zip and run its "Install SmartKeyboard.cmd".
  Your words and settings are kept.
- To remove it, run "Uninstall SmartKeyboard.cmd". Your words and settings
  are kept in %APPDATA%\SmartKeyboard in case you install it again; delete
  that folder too if you want them gone.
